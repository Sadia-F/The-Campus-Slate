About The Campus Slate

📰 Our Mission

The Campus Slate is the student-run newspaper of New York Institute of Technology's Old Westbury campus. Established in 1966, we have been serving the NYIT community for over 60 years, providing news, opinions, and creative content written by students, for students.

📝 What We Do

We publish monthly issues covering campus events, student life, academic achievements, and topics that matter to our community. Our team of writers, editors, photographers, and designers work together to create a publication that reflects the diverse voices of NYIT.
