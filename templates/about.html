About The Campus Slate

The Campus Slate is the student-run newspaper of New York Institute of Technology's Old Westbury campus. Since 1966, we have served the NYIT community with news, opinions, and creative content — thoroughly student-driven, written by students for students.

Campus, Countless Stories

📰 Our Mission

To inform, inspire, and hold our community accountable through honest, well-crafted journalism. We give a platform to perspectives that might otherwise go unheard — and we do it with the independence only a student-run publication can offer.

📝 What We Do

We publish monthly issues covering campus events, student life, academic achievements, and the topics that matter to our community. Writers, editors, photographers, designers, and videographers work together to produce a publication that reflects the true diversity of NYIT.

📜 Our History

1966

First Issue Published

The Campus Slate debuts, beginning over six decades of student journalism at NYIT's Old Westbury campus.

2024

A New Chapter

Prof. Kevin Horton joins as Faculty Advisor, and the publication begins its energetic rejuvenation under a new editorial team.

2025

Video Journalism Division

The Slate expands beyond print, launching a video journalism division to tell campus stories in new formats.

2026

A Digital Home of Our Own

A custom-built digital platform replaces paid web hosting — built by student engineers, for the whole community to enjoy.
